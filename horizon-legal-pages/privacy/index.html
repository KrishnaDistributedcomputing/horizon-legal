Horizon / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Horizon stores your teams, cities and working hours locally on your device. It has no developer-operated backend or account system. Optional location and address-search services may send information to Apple for processing, as explained below.

01About Horizon

Horizon is a free iOS app that helps people coordinate meeting times across time zones. It is developed by Krishnaswamy Venkataraman (also known as Krishna Venkataraman), referred to here as "the developer", "we" or "us".

02Information stored on your device

The teams, cities and working hours you enter are stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This information is not sent to the developer or to any server operated by Horizon.

Horizon has no developer-operated backend, account system or AI integration.

Optional location access and place search

If you grant permission, Apple's CoreLocation supplies your location while Horizon is in use in the foreground. Location permission is optional, and you can change or revoke it through iOS Settings.

You can select cities manually and use bundled place search without granting location access. Bundled place search remains available if geocoding fails.

Apple's CLGeocoder performs address search and reverse geocoding (turning a location into a place or address). These services may transmit location information or search text to Apple for processing under Apple's applicable policies. Local storage of your teams, cities and working hours does not mean that no information ever leaves your device.

03Calendar access

Horizon writes an event to your device calendar through Apple's EventKit framework only when you tap Add to Calendar. It does not add events at any other time.

Horizon does not upload your calendar data to a server.

04No purchases or paid features

Horizon is entirely free. It has no purchases, tips, subscriptions or paid features.

05No advertising, analytics or tracking

  • Horizon includes no advertising or advertising SDKs.
  • Horizon includes no analytics, including third-party analytics.
  • Horizon does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Its privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking as false.

06Notifications

If you enable them, Horizon may schedule optional local notifications using the iOS notification system. These are created on your device, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings.

07Children

Horizon is not directed at children under 13.

08Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. When it is, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.

09Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the App Support link on Horizon's App Store listing:

  • Open the Horizon listing in the App Store on your iPhone.
  • Find the App Support link on that listing.
  • Use the developer contact associated with the listing to reach Krishnaswamy Venkataraman directly.

Support for Horizon is provided by the developer, not by Apple.
